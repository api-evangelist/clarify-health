San Francisco, CA — May 1, 2026 Eleven years ago, Todd Gottula set out to build something healthcare had never seen. Today, he steps into the role of chief executive officer at Clarify Health Solutions and the work is just getting started. Todd has spent more than a decade assembling the most comprehensive longitudinal view of American healthcare ever created: six billion patient journeys per year, connecting every referral, every site of care, and every outcome across the system.
