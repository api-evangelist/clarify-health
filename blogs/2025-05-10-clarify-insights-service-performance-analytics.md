May 9th, 2025 Clarify Health’s performance analytics help organizations optimize provider networks, improve performance, and drive growth. Using proprietary methodologies like Patient Care Episode Groupings, PCP and Specialty Attribution, and patient-level benchmarking, they deliver actionable insights on provider performance, market rates, and referral patterns. Extending beyond the software, customers may augment their staff with hands-on support from Clarify’s analysts, enabling competitive network design, cost efficiency, and data-driven performance improvements to achieve strategic goals.
