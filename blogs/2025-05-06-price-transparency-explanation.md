May 5, 2025 Bring clarity to healthcare Price Transparency data Clarify Health’s Rates IQ Suite transforms complex healthcare price transparency data into actionable intelligence for payers and providers. By combining hospital and payer-reported rates with claims data from over 300 million patient lives, Clarify helps organizations benchmark reimbursement rates, evaluate provider performance, optimize networks, and negotiate with greater confidence. This overview explains how enriched pricing analytics support smarter contracting, cost management, and value-based care strategies.
