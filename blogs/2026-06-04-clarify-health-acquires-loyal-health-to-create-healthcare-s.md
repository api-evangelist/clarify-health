Combination unifies referral intelligence and quality analytics with purpose-built care activation, closing the gap between knowing where patients need care and moving them into it. SAN FRANCISCO — June 4, 2026 — Clarify Health ("Clarify"), a tech-enabled outcomes company delivering referral intelligence, quality measurement, and network performance solutions for health systems, today announced it has completed the acquisition of Loyal Health Holdings, Inc. ("Loyal"), a healthcare-specific patient activation platform.
