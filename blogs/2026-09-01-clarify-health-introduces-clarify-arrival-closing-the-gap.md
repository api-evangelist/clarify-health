Arrival guides patients to the right care, in the right place, at the right time. Working with Clarify Meridian™, it covers all three paths patients take into care and measures success on one outcome: the booked in-network appointment. SAN FRANCISCO—September 1, 2026 - Clarify Health today introduced Clarify Arrival ™, a patient activation solution that guides patients to the healthcare they need while helping the health system that serves them keep that care in network with the right providers.
