# Clarify Health

> Clarify Health Solutions is a healthcare analytics and outcomes company serving health
> systems, payers and life sciences organizations. Its platform — the Clarify Atlas
> Platform, surfaced to customers as Clarify Meridian — unifies referral intelligence,
> physician activation, consumer engagement and outcomes attribution on top of
> longitudinal claims and patient records. Clarify Health is backed by SoftBank Vision
> Fund and acquired Loyal Health in 2026.

## Agent-relevant summary

Clarify Health ships a SaaS analytics product, not a developer platform. As of 2026-08-15
there is **no public API, no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI or GraphQL
contract, no SDK on any package registry, and no A2A agent card**. An agent cannot
integrate with Clarify Health programmatically from public information. Access to the
Atlas / Meridian platform is a customer relationship established through the sales motion
at https://clarifyhealth.com/contact-clarify.

Generated by API Evangelist from the public Clarify Health surface. This file is not
published by Clarify Health; the provider's own /llms.txt returns HTTP 404.
